ANALYSIS STEP 1 & 2

Contract Types & Customer Tenure

See how contract duration and customer relationship length affect churn rates.

Churn Rate by Contract Type

Month-to-Month contracts have 15 times higher churn than 2-Year contracts.

Highest Impact
KEY FINDING

Month-to-month contracts (42.71% churn) account for over 88% of all lost customers. Switching customers to 1-Year (11.27%) or 2-Year (2.83%) contracts is the most effective way to keep them.

Churn Rate by Customer Tenure

New customers leave early: the average churner leaves within 10 months.

First-Year Risk
KEY FINDING

47.44% of churn happens in the first 12 months. After 24 months, churn drops to 14.5%, and after 4 years it drops to only 7.4%. Helping new customers in their first 90 days is critical.

ANALYSIS STEP 3 & 4

Monthly Bills & Payment Methods

See how pricing and payment methods influence customer retention.

Monthly Bill Ranges ($70 to $100 Risk Zone)

Churners pay an average of $74.44/mo vs $61.27/mo for loyal customers.

Pricing Impact
KEY FINDING

Customers paying $70 to $100 per month churn at 37.23%, mostly because Fiber Optic users feel the price is high when they lack technical support. Adding free perks helps make the price feel fair.

Payment Method Comparison

Electronic checks have nearly 3 times higher churn than automated credit card payments.

Autopay Advantage
KEY FINDING

Electronic check users churn at 45.29% because manual bill payment reminds them to consider canceling. Automatic bank or credit card payments cut churn down to 15.2% – 16.7%.

ANALYSIS STEP 8

4 Recommended Business Actions

Practical, data-backed steps the company can take to reduce churn and save revenue.

01
ACTION ONE

Incentivize Longer Contracts

Month-to-month contracts have 42.71% churn versus only 2.83% for 2-year contracts. Launch a simple campaign offering a 10% monthly discount or a free device upgrade when customers switch to a 1- or 2-year plan.

02
ACTION TWO

Support in the First 90 Days

Over 50% of lost customers leave within their first year (average 10 months). Call new customers in week 2 to ensure internet setup is working smoothly and send a satisfaction survey at month 3.

03
ACTION THREE

Promote Automatic Bill Pay

Electronic check users churn at 45.29%—nearly 3 times higher than credit card (15.2%) or bank transfer (16.7%). Offer a $5 monthly bill discount for setting up automatic payment, removing monthly bill friction.

04
ACTION FOUR

Bundle Tech Support with Fiber

Customers with monthly bills between $70 and $100 churn frequently because they lack Tech Support. Include free online security and priority phone support with Fiber Optic plans to boost perceived value.
